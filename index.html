Digital Credential Infrastructure

StatusPrototype
RegionCanada first
CredentialWork authorization

Proof you can work, held on your own phone and shown one claim at a time.

A work-authorization credential that lives in a wallet on the holder's device instead of as a scanned document in someone's inbox. A check gets the claims it needs, such as whether the person is authorized to work and until when, and nothing else. Built on open standards so it isn't tied to one wallet or one issuer.

OpenID4VCI · X.509Issuer

Signs the credential and issues it straight to the holder's wallet.

ISO/IEC 18013-5 mdocHolder

Keeps it on the device, bound to keys that never leave it, and picks which claims to release.

OpenID4VP · X.509Verifier

Requests specific claims, checks the issuer's signature, and receives only what the holder approved.

05

How we work

Four rules that apply to everything we ship.

  1. Evidence sits under the claim.

    The assistant never states a number without the chart it came from.

  2. Nothing you can fail.

    No streaks, no red days, no score you can lose.

  3. On the device wherever possible.

    Processing stays on your phone by default.

  4. Two care systems from day one.

    Designed in Vancouver and tested against both Japanese and North American clinical practice.

Rule four, in the app. Step 2 of onboarding in Japan asks for consent under APPI before any health data is collected. Brazil gets its own consent screen under LGPD.
