How we work · 04

Four rules we
don't bend.

They started as interface rules in the app. They turned out to describe the company.

Evidence sits under the claim

The assistant never states a number without the chart it came from. Neither do we — no metric on this site that we can't show you the working for.

Nothing you can fail

No streaks, no red days, no score you can lose. A measurement out of range earns a sentence, not an alarm.

On the device wherever possible

Processing stays on your phone by default. Exports leave AI inference switched off, because a clinician asked for measurements.

Two care systems from day one

Designed in Vancouver and tested against both Japanese and North American clinical practice, rather than localised after the fact.
